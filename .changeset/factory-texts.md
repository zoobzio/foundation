The Nuxt layer resolves a label where the component renders it. Every component of `@zoobzio/vue` now exposes a slot around each text it draws — the select's and multi-select's `itemLabel`, the listbox's `itemLabel`, the tabs' and the accordion's `triggerLabel`, the hero's `actionLabel`, the form field's `labelText` and `hintText`, the tree's `itemText`, and the date filters' `fieldLabel`, `operatorLabel` and `valueLabel` over a context that carries the stepper's `field`, `operator` and `value` — and the select, multi-select and autocomplete leave an item's typeahead text to what the item renders. The layer's wrappers fill those slots with the text in the visitor's locale and hand their props through untouched, save a scalar the component gives to an attribute (a placeholder, an accessible name); the `texts` helper that resolved a wrapper's props by walking them is gone. The factories resolve the whole config of a table, browser or form in one call, rather than the columns, actions and fields by name, so a text anywhere in the config — a literal's label, say — resolves as a column's does; the form's `defaults`, the payload's values, stand as given. Two defaults the headless packages ship in English have messages in `@zoobzio/prose` and are laid under the config when it names none: `table.text`, the label of the query's text literal, and `browser.root`, the label of the browser's root crumb. `@zoobzio/browser` carries `rootLabel` and a crumb's `label` as `Text`, as it carries a column's.
