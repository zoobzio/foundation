The autocomplete's active panel scrolls in the package's scroller, and its virtualized items render within a row the component owns. `viewport` in `AutocompletePassthrough` is now `Rendered<ScrollerProps>`: the scroller renders through the package's `Primitive`, so a `pt.viewport` takes the scroller's props and `pt`, and `as` swaps the component, as the nuxt layer does with its own Scroller. The scroller's viewport renders the virtualizer as its child, so the virtualizer scrolls with it; a bounded height on the scroll area's viewport is what makes the panel scroll, and the scrollbar is the scroller's to style. The virtualizer positions a row by its root and reka's item memoizes its element on its own state alone, so a row's corrected position, once measured, landed only when the row was next highlighted; consumers worked around it with an exact `estimateSize`. Each row is now a plain `.f-autocomplete-row` element the virtualizer positions and measures, with the item inside, so a measured height moves the rows after it and the estimate only seeds the first layout. An `item` slot on the active panel renders within that row: it may render any content, and `measure` is gone from its payload. The default item still carries `aria-setsize` and `aria-posinset`. In the nuxt layer, `Autocomplete` is wrapped with the layer's Scroller as its viewport, and the table's search renders through it.
