Icons. A new `@zoobzio/icons` package carries the foundation icon contract: the roles the components of `@zoobzio/vue` leave to their `*Icon` slots (`prev`, `next`, `close`, `search`, `sort-asc`, …), authored as an icon-sheets config and built by `@icon-sheets/kit` into a typed `Alias` union, a resolved contract, and sprites.

The Nuxt layer loads `@icon-sheets/nuxt` with that contract as its default `iconSheets` option, so an app that extends the layer gets `<Icon name="…" />` and the sprite with no setup, and an app with its own icons package merges its contract over the foundation's by passing it the usual way. `#imports` carries `IconAlias`, the app's merged alias union.

`@zoobzio/vue` gains `wrap` (`utils/wrap`): a component with a `pt` layer and slot defaults, typed as the component itself, for a framework layer to stand in for the component with its own parts and defaults laid under the consumer's. The item types (`MenuItem`, `TabsOption`, `SegmentedControlOption`, `BreadcrumbItem`, `DirectoryItem`, `CommandOption`, `FacetItem`, and the action and crumb items over them) are interfaces now, so a layer may widen one by declaration merging; `TreeNode` stays an alias for reka's record bound. `useExplicit` is gone: it served the layer's former wrappers, which `wrap` replaces.

The `*Icon` slots are gone from every component of `@zoobzio/vue` (`triggerIcon`, `itemIcon`, `prevIcon`, `nextIcon`, `closeIcon`, `sortIcon`, `refreshIcon`, and the rest, 48 in all). In their place each site has a `prepend` and an `append` slot around the content, with the scope the icon slot had: `itemPrepend`/`itemAppend` (menu, command, directory, segmented control, breadcrumb, tree, autocomplete), `triggerPrepend`/`triggerAppend` (select, multi-select, tabs, accordion, facets, keywords, date filters, the pickers), `prevPrepend`/`prevAppend` and `nextPrepend`/`nextAppend` (calendars and pickers), `firstPrepend`…`lastAppend` (pagination), `closePrepend`/`closeAppend` (toast), `itemDeletePrepend`/`itemDeleteAppend` (tags input), `itemRemovePrepend`/`itemRemoveAppend` (file input), `backToTopPrepend`/`backToTopAppend` (scroller), `toolPrepend`/`toolAppend` (editor), `headerPrepend`/`headerAppend` and `dragPrepend`/`dragAppend` (table head), `bulkActionPrepend`/`bulkActionAppend` (bulk actions), `columnsPrepend`/`columnsAppend` (column manager), `refreshPrepend`/`refreshAppend` (table, browser), `actionsPrepend`/`actionsAppend` and `actionPrepend`/`actionAppend` (table rows, browser files), `folderPrepend`/`folderAppend`, `folderActionsPrepend`/`folderActionsAppend`, and `folderActionPrepend`/`folderActionAppend` (browser folders), and `prepend`/`append` on the Fab. A site that showed placeholder text under its icon slot (the pickers' "Calendar", "Previous", and "Next", the toast's "Close", the tag and file "Remove") keeps the text between the pair. The Fab's and the checkbox's own `icon` slots stay.

The Nuxt layer's wrappers are each one `wrap` call, so the nine link wrappers are a `pt` line each. The menu is the first component to draw icons: the layer widens `MenuItem` with an optional `icon: IconAlias` in its `app.d.ts`, and the menu wrapper fills `itemPrepend` with that icon by default; a consumer's own `itemPrepend` slot still wins.
