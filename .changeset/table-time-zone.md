The table and browser configs take a `timeZone`, the IANA zone their `date` and `datetime` cells format in, beside `locale` and `currency`. It defaults to `UTC`, so a server and a browser render the same text and hydration no longer depends on the process zone. A calendar day, `YYYY-MM-DD`, always formats in UTC: a day has no zone to shift it into the one before. `cell` in `@zoobzio/vue` takes the zone as its fifth argument.
