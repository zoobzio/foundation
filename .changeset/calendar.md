A calendar. `@zoobzio/calendar` is a new headless service over JSCalendar 2.0 entries (draft-ietf-calext-jscalendarbis-22), events and tasks as plain JSON, exported as types under `JSCalendar`. It owns the view (`month`, `week`, `day`, `agenda`), the date, the range they span, the entries the range loaded, and the occurrences they recur into: a rule expands through rrule-temporal, overrides add, change and exclude occurrences, a wall-clock time holds across a clock change, and each occurrence is a JSCalendar object of its own. `fetch(range)` loads through the reader; `create`, `update(key, patch, scope)` and `remove(key, scope)` change an entry, one occurrence (`one`, into `recurrenceOverrides`), or an occurrence and every later one (`following`, the entry cut in two with `relatedTo` between them), and persist through `save`, which puts the entries back when it rejects. It emits `navigated`, `loaded`, `changed`, and `selected`. The vue `calendar.vue` renders it (`defineCalendar`, the `Calendar*` types, `useCalendar`, the `f-data-calendar-*` classes): a toolbar built from the service as the editor's is, one tool per command in groups inside reka's toolbar (`today`, `prev`, `next`, then a toggle per view), with the `tool` part, the `tool`, `toolPrepend`, `toolLabel`, and `toolAppend` slots per tool, and `toolbarPrepend` and `toolbarAppend` for a consumer's own controls, over a table of the view's days, or the agenda's list, each day with the occurrences that touch it. The nuxt layer adds `createCalendar`, `accessCalendar`, the `calendar:*` hooks, and a wrapper that names each tool in the visitor's locale and draws `prev` and `next` as icons by the tool's key (`CALENDAR_TOOL_ICONS`, `CALENDAR_TOOL_TEXT`), with the rest of its text in the visitor's locale (`calendar.today`, `calendar.previous`, `calendar.next`, `calendar.month`, `calendar.week`, `calendar.day`, `calendar.agenda`, `calendar.untitled`, `calendar.empty`, new in the prose contract).
