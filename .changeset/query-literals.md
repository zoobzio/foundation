The query's text stage is generalized into literals: a value typed between its own `open` and `close` characters, with a label for its option and no stage to suggest from. The config's `literals` are keyed as its fields are, and default to the text literal alone, `text`: free text between quotes under `Search`, the one `text` reads, so a word that is also a field's label is never taken for one. The field panel leads with the literal options while nothing is typed; selecting one writes its `open` before the term typed so far. The literal stage is terminal: it suggests nothing and adds no trail, so the panels are empty while the literal is typed and a panel view closes, where the text stage held its option open in the trail. Typing the `close` commits the literal at once, as Enter does an unclosed one; a bare term submits nothing, as it names a field still. A literal entry is `{ kind: "literal", key, value }`, one held per key, in place of the `text` entry kind; `textFor` of one is its value between the characters, the string `labelFor` gives and `unwrap` hands back minus the last character. `literals: {}` replaces `text: false`: there is no literal option and a quote is a character. `Literal` is an interface a layer may widen, its other properties carried onto its option as a field's are; `QUERY_TEXT_LITERAL`, `QUERY_LITERALS`, and `QUERY_TEXT_KEY` replace the quote and label constants. The table's config takes `literals` in place of `textLabel` and hands them to its query. The vue autocomplete renders nothing, not even its empty state, in a literal stage. The nuxt layer widens `Literal` with `icon`, draws `search` before a literal option that names none, and no arrow after one, as it is terminal.
